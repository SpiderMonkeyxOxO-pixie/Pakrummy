## Direct answer

A withdrawal usually shows as pending due to identity verification still being processed, normal payment-processor delays, or the app's own stated processing window simply not having elapsed yet. If it exceeds that stated window with no explanation, that's when to contact support rather than keep waiting.

## What "pending" actually means

"Pending" generally means the request has been received but the money hasn't reached your account yet. It isn't the same as "rejected" or "failed", and on its own it doesn't tell you something has gone wrong. It only means one or more steps are still in progress.

## Common reasons a withdrawal stays pending

- **Identity verification isn't finished.** If an app requires KYC before the first withdrawal, the request is commonly held until verification is complete. Our [KYC explainer](/blog/what-does-kyc-verification-mean/) covers this in general terms.
- **The processing window hasn't elapsed.** Apps state an expected processing time. Until that has passed, a pending status is normal.
- **Payment-processor delays.** Wallets and banks have their own processing times and occasional downtime, which sit outside the app's control.
- **Details that don't match.** A payment account name or number that doesn't match the verified account holder can cause a hold or rejection.
- **Account or transaction review.** Platforms may review some withdrawals manually, which can take longer than automated ones.

## How long is normal?

This varies by app and payment method, so the most reliable figure is the one the app itself states. For Pak Rummy, the processing time we have confirmed is on our [withdrawal guide](/withdrawal/), along with the note that it can be longer if verification is pending. We don't promise a specific resolution time beyond that.

## What to check first

1. Compare how long you've waited against the app's own stated processing window.
2. Check whether your account verification is complete. See [account verification](/account-verification/).
3. Confirm the payment account details you entered are correct and belong to you.
4. Check the withdrawal status in the app for any message explaining the hold.

## When to escalate

If a withdrawal goes beyond the app's stated processing window with no explanation, stop waiting and use a formal channel. Keep your transaction details and the time of the request ready. See our [complaints page](/complaints/) for how to raise it.
