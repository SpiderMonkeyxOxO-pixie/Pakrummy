## Direct answer

To withdraw money from JazzCash to your bank account, open the app, select "Send Money" or "Withdraw" (the exact label depends on your app version), choose Raast bank transfer, debit card cash-out, or an agent, and confirm the amount. Raast transfers to a supporting bank are usually the fastest; other methods depend on your bank or the agent's own processing.

## Funding-out methods

JazzCash generally supports a few ways to move money out of your Mobile Account (MA) balance and into your bank or into cash:

| Method | What it is | Typical speed | What you need |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Raast bank transfer** | A direct transfer from your JazzCash Mobile Account to a bank account via Raast, Pakistan's interbank instant payment system | Usually near-instant where both ends support it | A bank account at a Raast-participating bank, linked and verified in the JazzCash app |
| **Debit card** | Cash-out to a JazzCash Mobile Account card or a linked bank debit card | Varies by card issuer and account tier | A JazzCash MA debit card or a linked bank debit card added in-app |
| **Agent cash-out** | Withdrawing cash in person through a JazzCash retail agent | Depends on the agent processing it on their end | Your JazzCash account details and ID, presented at the agent point |

Which of these is available to you depends on your account tier, your bank's own Raast participation, and your verification level — the same factors that govern deposits (see our [JazzCash deposit guide](/blog/how-to-deposit-money-into-jazzcash/)).

## Step-by-step: Raast bank transfer

1. Open the JazzCash app and select **Send Money** or **Withdraw to Bank** (label varies by app version).
2. Choose **Raast** as the transfer method.
3. Select or add the destination bank account — it must be linked and, where required, verified in-app.
4. Enter the amount you want to withdraw.
5. Confirm the transaction, including any MPIN or OTP step JazzCash requires.
6. Wait for confirmation — Raast transfers are typically near-instant when both your JazzCash account and the receiving bank support it.

## Step-by-step: debit card cash-out

1. Open the JazzCash app and select the debit card option under **Withdraw** or **Send Money**.
2. Choose the JazzCash Mobile Account card or a linked bank debit card.
3. Enter the amount and confirm the transaction with your MPIN or OTP.
4. Wait for the transaction to post — timing depends on your card issuer and account tier, not on JazzCash alone.

## Step-by-step: agent cash-out

1. Locate a JazzCash retail agent.
2. Present your registered mobile number and valid ID matching your JazzCash account details.
3. Tell the agent the amount you want to withdraw.
4. Confirm the transaction on your end, typically via MPIN or an OTP sent to your registered number.
5. Receive your cash and keep the transaction receipt or ID until you've confirmed your JazzCash balance updated correctly.

## Before you withdraw

- **Confirm your account verification tier.** A basic Mobile Account typically has lower withdrawal limits than a fully CNIC-verified one. See our [account verification page](/account-verification/) for what identity verification generally involves.
- **Match your registered details.** The bank account, card, or ID you use should match the name and details registered to your JazzCash account — mismatches are a common cause of a failed or held withdrawal.
- **Check the fee shown on your own screen.** Withdrawal charges vary by amount, destination, and account tier — see [JazzCash withdrawal charges: what to expect](/blog/jazzcash-withdrawal-charges/) for how that generally works, and confirm the exact figure in-app before approving.
- **Keep your transaction ID and timestamp** until the withdrawal is confirmed and reflects in your bank account or cash in hand.

## FAQ

**What methods can I use to withdraw money from JazzCash?**
Raast bank transfer, a debit card (JazzCash's own Mobile Account card or a linked bank card), or in-person cash-out through a JazzCash agent — availability depends on your account tier and bank.

**Is a Raast bank withdrawal faster than other methods?**
Where your bank supports it, yes — Raast is Pakistan's interbank instant payment system, so a Raast withdrawal to a participating bank typically settles faster than a card or agent cash-out. Confirm your bank supports Raast first.

**Why would my withdrawal be delayed or capped?**
Incomplete identity verification is a common cause, separate from any fee question — a basic Mobile Account has lower limits than a fully verified one. See our [account verification page](/account-verification/).

**Where can I check the exact fee for my withdrawal?**
Check the figure shown on your own JazzCash withdrawal screen at the time of the transaction.
