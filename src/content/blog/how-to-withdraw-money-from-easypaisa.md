## Direct answer

To withdraw money from Easypaisa to your bank account, open the app, select "Send Money" or "Withdraw" (the exact label depends on your app version), choose bank transfer, debit card cash-out, or an agent, and confirm the amount. A bank transfer to a linked account is usually the most predictable option; other methods depend on your card issuer or the agent's own processing.

## Funding-out methods

Easypaisa generally supports a few ways to move money out of your account balance and into your bank or into cash:

| Method | What it is | Typical speed | What you need |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bank transfer** | A transfer from your Easypaisa balance to a linked bank account | Often near-instant, but can vary by receiving bank | A bank account linked and, where required, verified in the Easypaisa app |
| **Debit card** | Cash-out to an Easypaisa debit card or a linked bank debit card | Varies by card issuer and account tier | An Easypaisa debit card or a linked bank debit card added in-app |
| **Agent cash-out** | Withdrawing cash in person through an Easypaisa retail agent | Depends on the agent processing it on their end | Your Easypaisa account details and ID, presented at the agent point |

Which of these is available to you depends on your account tier, your bank's own setup, and your verification level — the same factors that govern deposits (see our [Easypaisa deposit guide](/blog/how-to-deposit-money-into-easypaisa/)).

## Step-by-step: bank transfer

1. Open the Easypaisa app and select **Send Money** or **Withdraw to Bank** (label varies by app version).
2. Choose **bank transfer** as the method.
3. Select or add the destination bank account — it must be linked and, where required, verified in-app.
4. Enter the amount you want to withdraw.
5. Confirm the transaction, including any PIN or OTP step Easypaisa requires.
6. Wait for confirmation — processing time depends on your receiving bank as well as Easypaisa.

## Step-by-step: debit card cash-out

1. Open the Easypaisa app and select the debit card option under **Withdraw** or **Send Money**.
2. Choose the Easypaisa debit card or a linked bank debit card.
3. Enter the amount and confirm the transaction with your PIN or OTP.
4. Wait for the transaction to post — timing depends on your card issuer and account tier, not on Easypaisa alone.

## Step-by-step: agent cash-out

1. Locate an Easypaisa retail agent.
2. Present your registered mobile number and valid ID matching your Easypaisa account details.
3. Tell the agent the amount you want to withdraw.
4. Confirm the transaction on your end, typically via PIN or an OTP sent to your registered number.
5. Receive your cash and keep the transaction receipt or ID until you've confirmed your Easypaisa balance updated correctly.

## Before you withdraw

- **Confirm your account verification tier.** A basic account typically has lower withdrawal limits than a fully verified one. See our [account verification page](/account-verification/) for what identity verification generally involves.
- **Match your registered details.** The bank account, card, or ID you use should match the name and details registered to your Easypaisa account — mismatches are a common cause of a failed or held withdrawal.
- **Check the fee shown on your own screen.** Withdrawal charges vary by amount, destination, and account tier — see [Easypaisa withdrawal charges: what to expect](/blog/easypaisa-withdrawal-charges/) for how that generally works, and confirm the exact figure in-app before approving.
- **Keep your transaction ID and timestamp** until the withdrawal is confirmed and reflects in your bank account or cash in hand.

## FAQ

**What methods can I use to withdraw money from Easypaisa?**
Bank transfer, a debit card (Easypaisa's own debit card or a linked bank card), or in-person cash-out through an Easypaisa agent — availability depends on your account tier and bank.

**Does Easypaisa support Raast for withdrawals?**
We haven't confirmed that. This guide covers the withdrawal methods Easypaisa has itself confirmed — bank transfer, debit card, and agent cash-out — rather than assuming a specific transfer rail applies.

**Why would my withdrawal be delayed or capped?**
Incomplete identity verification is a common cause, separate from any fee question — a basic account has lower limits than a fully verified one. See our [account verification page](/account-verification/).

**Where can I check the exact fee for my withdrawal?**
Check the figure shown on your own Easypaisa withdrawal screen at the time of the transaction. For how Easypaisa's fee structure generally works and where to find its official Schedule of Charges, see [Easypaisa withdrawal charges: what to expect](/blog/easypaisa-withdrawal-charges/).
