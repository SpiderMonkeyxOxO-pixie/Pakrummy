## Direct answer

Teen Patti is a 3-card betting game: everyone antes into a pot, each player gets 3 cards face-down, and the highest-ranking hand wins — unless everyone else folds first. Players bet "blind" (without looking at their cards) or "seen" (after looking), and the hand rankings run from Trail (three of a kind) down to High Card.

## Setup and dealing

Before any cards are dealt, every player puts a fixed minimum stake — the boot amount — into the pot in the middle of the table. Each player then receives 3 cards face-down. Play moves clockwise, one betting round at a time, until either one player remains or the hand goes to a showdown between the last two players.

## Blind vs. seen play

This is the mechanic that trips up most new players. A **blind** player keeps their cards face-down and doesn't look at them — they bet without knowing their hand, but at a lower stake. A **seen** player looks at their cards first, and from that point on must bet at least double what a blind player bets. The trade-off is simple: playing blind is cheaper per round but riskier, since you're betting without information; playing seen costs more but lets you fold early on a weak hand instead of burning chips on it blind.

## Hand rankings, highest to lowest

| Rank | Hand | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Trail** (three of a kind) | 7-7-7 |
| 2 | **Pure Sequence** (straight flush) | 4-5-6, all same suit |
| 3 | **Sequence** (straight) | 4-5-6, mixed suits |
| 4 | **Color** (flush) | 2-7-J, all same suit, not in order |
| 5 | **Pair** | 9-9-4 |
| 6 | **High Card** | No pair, sequence, or color — compare the highest card |

Aces rank highest in a Trail (A-A-A beats K-K-K), but lowest when used at the bottom of a sequence (A-2-3). If two players both hold the same hand type, the one with the higher-ranking cards wins.

## Betting actions

On your turn, you can:

- **Call** — match the current bet to stay in the hand.
- **Raise** — increase the bet, forcing others to match it or fold.
- **Fold (pack)** — withdraw from the hand and forfeit anything already bet.
- **Show** — when only two players remain, either can request a showdown by paying double the current stake; both hands are revealed and the higher hand takes the pot.
