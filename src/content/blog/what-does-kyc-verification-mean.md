## Direct answer

KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is the identity-check process gaming apps use to confirm who holds an account, typically requiring a government-issued ID, before releasing withdrawals. It exists to reduce fraud and protect both the player and the platform.

## What KYC actually is

"Know Your Customer" is a general term for the checks a financial or real-money service runs to confirm that an account belongs to a real, identifiable person. In a gaming app, it usually means submitting proof of identity once, so the operator can match the account to a person before money leaves it.

## Why gaming apps require it

- **Fraud reduction.** Confirming identity makes it harder for someone to withdraw funds from an account they don't own.
- **Account-takeover protection.** If someone gains access to your login, a verification step before withdrawal adds a second barrier.
- **Age confirmation.** An ID check is how an operator confirms an account holder is old enough to play.

## What documents are typically requested

Common practice in this category includes:

- A government-issued ID, such as a CNIC in Pakistan.
- Sometimes a selfie or short liveness check, to confirm the ID belongs to the person using the account.
- A name on your payment method that matches the name on your ID.

Exact requirements vary by app, so treat this as a typical pattern, not a fixed list.

## When verification usually happens

This varies by app. Many allow you to play before verifying but require it before the first withdrawal. If you haven't verified, that is commonly the reason a withdrawal request is held or rejected, so completing it early avoids delays later.

## If verification is rejected

Common fixes are a well-lit, uncropped, unexpired ID photo, an exact name match between your ID and payment method, and a retaken selfie in even lighting. If it is still rejected, contact customer service with your registered number.

## FAQ

**Why do gaming apps require KYC at all?**
To confirm the account holder's identity before releasing funds, reducing fraud and account-takeover risk.

**What documents are typically requested?**
A government-issued ID (such as a CNIC in Pakistan) and sometimes a selfie or proof-of-ownership step. Exact requirements vary by app.

**Does KYC have to happen before I can play, or only before I withdraw?**
This varies by app. Many allow play before verification but require it before the first withdrawal.
