## Direct answer

JazzCash withdrawal charges depend on the transaction amount, destination, and the receiving app's fee policy, and are not a single fixed rate. Confirm the exact charge on your withdrawal screen, and note that identity verification status can delay a withdrawal independently of any fee.

## Why there's no single answer

JazzCash is a mobile wallet, and money can leave it in several ways: to a bank account, to another wallet, or as cash through an agent. Each route can carry its own fee, and some wallets tier those fees by amount bracket. The app you're withdrawing from may also apply its own processing fee, separate from anything JazzCash charges. A figure quoted on a forum or in a video may be out of date or may describe a different route than yours, so treat it as a rough guide, not a promise.

## What can change the charge you see

- **Transaction amount.** Smaller and larger withdrawals can fall into different fee brackets.
- **Destination.** Moving money to a bank account, to another wallet, or to cash at an agent are different services.
- **The app's own fee policy.** The app facilitating the withdrawal may add its own charge on top of the wallet's.
- **Account type and verification level.** Wallet accounts commonly come in tiers, and a fully verified account can have different limits from a basic one. Check which tier your own JazzCash account is on and what it allows, since we can't tell you from the outside.

## Fees and delays are separate problems

A withdrawal that takes longer than expected isn't necessarily a fee issue. Incomplete or mismatched identity verification is a common cause of delay, and it can hold up a payout whatever the fee turns out to be. If your withdrawal is slow, check your verification status first. See our [account verification page](/account-verification/) for what we currently know.

## What to check before you confirm

1. Read the exact fee on the confirmation screen before you approve. Don't rely on a figure you remember from last time.
2. Make sure the name and number on the JazzCash account match the details registered with the app.
3. If the amount received is lower than expected, compare the gap against a disclosed fee. A disclosed fee and an unexplained shortfall call for different next steps.
4. Keep the transaction ID and timestamp until the money shows up in your account.
