## Direct answer

Real money earning games in Pakistan generally fall into three categories: skill-based card games like Rummy, Teen Patti, and Poker; prediction/multiplier games like Aviator and Crash; and chance-based games like Slots. Legal status and platform trustworthiness vary and should be checked independently of game type.

## Why the category matters

"Real money earning game" is a marketing phrase, not a technical category. Two games under that label can work in completely different ways: in one, your decisions meaningfully change the outcome; in another, the result is mostly random and your only decision is how much to stake and when to stop. Knowing which kind you are looking at helps you judge the risk before any money is involved.

## Skill-based card games

Rummy, Teen Patti, and Poker are card games played against other players. Decisions such as what to keep, what to discard, when to bet and when to fold affect results, though luck of the deal still plays a part. See our guides to [13-card Rummy](/blog/13-card-rummy-rules-beginners-walkthrough/), [Teen Patti](/blog/teen-patti-rules-beginners-guide/), and [Poker](/blog/poker-rules-for-beginners/) for how each is played.

## Prediction and multiplier games

Aviator and Crash-style games show a multiplier that rises until it suddenly stops. The player chooses when to cash out, and loses the stake if the round ends first. The timing decision is yours, but the point at which the round ends is not something a player controls. We explain the mechanics in [how Aviator works](/blog/how-aviator-game-works/) and [how Crash games work](/blog/how-crash-games-work/).

## Chance-based games (Slots)

Slots are games of chance: the outcome of each spin is determined by the game, not by player decisions. The player's only choices are the stake size and whether to keep playing.

## How the three categories differ

- **Player influence:** highest in card games, limited to cash-out timing in prediction games, and minimal in slots.
- **Opponents:** card games are typically played against other players; prediction and slot games are played against the game itself.
- **Pace:** prediction and slot rounds are usually much faster than a hand of cards, which can make spending harder to track.
