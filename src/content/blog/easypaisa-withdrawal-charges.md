## Direct answer

Easypaisa withdrawal charges vary by transaction type, destination, and the receiving app's own fee policy — there is no single flat rate across all services. Always confirm the exact charge shown on your own withdrawal screen before confirming the transaction.

## Why there is no single Easypaisa fee

"Easypaisa withdrawal charges" sounds like one number, but in practice it is several different charges that depend on what you are doing. Moving money from a gaming or wallet app into an Easypaisa account, taking cash out at an agent, and sending money onward to a bank account are different transaction types, and each can be priced differently. Fee schedules can also change over time, so a figure quoted on a forum or in a video may be out of date by the time you read it.

## Where a charge can come from

When a withdrawal lands lower than you expected, the difference can come from more than one place:

1. **Easypaisa itself.** The wallet provider may deduct a charge depending on the transaction type or the destination.
2. **The app you are withdrawing from.** An app may apply its own processing fee, or none at all.
3. **The receiving bank or agent.** If the money moves onward from your wallet, a further charge may apply at that step.

Check the fee schedule at each stage, not just one of them.

## Flat fee or percentage?

It depends on the transaction type and the receiving app or bank. Some charges are a fixed amount, others scale with the amount moved, and some are tiered by bracket. We are not going to give you a specific figure here, because a number we cannot verify today is worse than no number.

## What to check before you confirm

- **The figure on your own screen.** The withdrawal confirmation screen is the most reliable source for what will be deducted on that transaction.
- **The amount you will actually receive**, not just the amount you requested.
- **Your account tier and limits** in the Easypaisa app, since these can affect what a transaction costs and whether it goes through.
- **Your identity verification status.** Incomplete verification can delay a withdrawal separately from any fee question — see [account verification](/account-verification/).
