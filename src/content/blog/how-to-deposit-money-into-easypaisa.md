## Direct answer

To deposit money into Easypaisa, open the app, select "Add Money," then choose a funding source — bank transfer, debit card, or an agent cash deposit — and confirm the amount. Processing time depends on the method chosen.

## Before you start

Funding an Easypaisa account is general wallet mechanics, not something specific to any gaming app. Menu names and screens can change between app versions, so treat the steps below as a general outline and follow what your own app shows. Your account tier also affects which methods and limits are available to you.

## Step-by-step: adding money

1. Open the Easypaisa app and sign in to your account.
2. Select "Add Money" (the label may differ slightly in your version).
3. Choose a funding source: bank transfer, debit card, or cash deposit through an agent.
4. Enter the amount and review any details shown on the confirmation screen.
5. Confirm, then keep the transaction ID and timestamp until the balance reflects in your wallet.

## Which funding source to choose

- **Bank transfer.** Convenient if your bank account is already linked; timing depends on your bank and the transfer method.
- **Debit card.** Usually quick, but depends on your card being enabled for this type of payment.
- **Agent cash deposit.** Useful if you prefer cash; confirm the agent is an authorised Easypaisa agent and keep your receipt.

Availability of each method depends on your account tier.

## Using a funded wallet for app deposits

Once your Easypaisa balance is funded, you can use it for payments to apps that support it. Check the amount, recipient and any fee on the confirmation screen before approving.
