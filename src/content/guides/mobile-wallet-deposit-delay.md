## Direct answer

Most delayed mobile-wallet payments resolve within minutes to a couple of
hours because of processing queues on the wallet or aggregator side, not
because the money has been lost. Confirm the debit actually happened, wait
through the platform's stated processing window, then escalate with your
transaction ID if it still hasn't cleared.

## Step by step

1. **Check your wallet app first.** Open Easypaisa or JazzCash and confirm the
   transaction shows as **Successful**, not **Pending** or **Failed**. A
   pending debit on the wallet side means the platform likely hasn't received
   confirmation yet either.
2. **Note the transaction ID and timestamp.** You'll need both for any support
   conversation — screenshot the confirmation SMS or in-app receipt.
3. **Wait through the platform's stated window.** Processing times vary by
   operator and time of day; see our [Easypaisa and JazzCash guide](/easypaisa-jazzcash/)
   for what we've been able to confirm for Pak Rummy specifically.
4. **Restart the app and check your balance again** — some balance displays
   cache briefly and don't refresh until you reopen the app or pull to refresh.
5. **Contact support with evidence, not just a description.** Provide the
   transaction ID, amount, timestamp, and a screenshot. See
   [customer service](/customer-service/) for how to reach out.
6. **Do not attempt the same deposit twice** while the first is still pending
   — that makes reconciliation harder and can cause a genuine duplicate charge.

## Common causes of a delay

- Wallet-side processing queues during high-traffic periods.
- Aggregator or payment-gateway confirmation lag between the wallet and the
  platform.
- A typo in the account or reference number entered during payment.
- Network interruption after the debit but before the confirmation callback.

## When to treat it as a dispute rather than a delay

If the wallet clearly shows a successful debit and the platform still hasn't
credited the amount after the stated processing window has passed, treat it
as a support case rather than continuing to wait — bring your transaction ID
and timestamp to [customer service](/customer-service/).
