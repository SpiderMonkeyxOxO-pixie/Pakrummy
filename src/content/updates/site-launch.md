## What changed

PakRummyOfficial.com launched today as an independent information, download-
verification, and support resource covering the Pak Rummy app for players in
Pakistan. This is a same-day, first-party fact about this website — it is not
a claim about the Pak Rummy app itself.

## What we could verify at launch

- The existence and general product category of the Pak Rummy brand (a
  13-card Rummy real-money gaming app aimed at Pakistan).
- Publicly common Pakistani payment rails in this category (Easypaisa,
  JazzCash) as a general category fact — not as a confirmed Pak Rummy
  integration. See [Easypaisa and JazzCash](/easypaisa-jazzcash/).

## What we could not verify at launch

We have not yet received or independently confirmed: an official download
URL and checksum, the current app version and file size, the operator's
registered legal name and support channels, current bonus terms, or which
games beyond Rummy are actually available in the app. Every page that
depends on one of these facts says so explicitly and disables the affected
call to action rather than guessing. The full list lives in
[our verification tracker](/safety/) and internal
`docs/required-verification.md`.

## Next steps

As operator-confirmed information becomes available, we'll publish a new
entry here describing exactly what changed and how it was verified.
