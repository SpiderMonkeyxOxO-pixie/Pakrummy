## Direct answer

Easypaisa and JazzCash both set transaction and balance limits based on your account tier, not a single fixed number for everyone — a basic, unverified account has lower limits than a fully verified one. The exact figures are set by each wallet and change periodically, so the only reliable place to check your current limit is inside your own account.

## Why account tiers exist

Mobile wallets in Pakistan operate under State Bank of Pakistan branchless-banking regulations, which require different levels of identity verification (KYC) for different transaction volumes — this is a regulatory structure, not a wallet-specific choice. In practice this usually means:

- **Basic / Level 0 account** — opened with minimal information (often just a mobile number), with low transaction and balance caps.
- **Verified / upgraded account** — opened or upgraded with a government-issued ID (CNIC) and biometric or in-person verification, unlocking higher limits.

Where exactly your account sits, and what the specific caps are at each tier, is set by the wallet provider and can change — see our note on [KYC verification](/blog/what-does-kyc-verification-mean/) for why this verification step exists in the first place.

## What limits typically apply to

- **Per-transaction maximum** — the largest single deposit, withdrawal, or transfer allowed in one go.
- **Daily maximum** — a cap on total transaction volume across a day, separate from the per-transaction cap.
- **Monthly maximum** — a longer-window cap, often tied directly to your verification tier.
- **Maximum wallet balance** — how much the wallet itself can hold at rest, regardless of transaction activity.

Not every wallet enforces all four the same way, and the exact numbers are genuinely a moving target — any specific figure you see quoted online, including a figure that was accurate last year, may not be current today.

## Where to check your actual current limits

Both wallets show your account's specific limits inside the app itself, usually under account settings or a "limits" section, and both publish their own official schedule of charges and limits directly — that's the only source we'd point you to over a secondhand roundup. See our [Easypaisa and JazzCash page](/easypaisa-jazzcash/) for links to each provider's own official documentation.

## Why this matters if you're using a wallet for gaming-app deposits or withdrawals

If a deposit or withdrawal fails or gets capped unexpectedly, your account's current tier and limit — not necessarily the gaming app itself — is one of the first things worth checking, especially if you're new to the wallet or recently increased how much you're moving through it. Raising your tier through verification, if you haven't already, is usually the direct fix rather than a workaround.

## FAQ

**Does upgrading my wallet account tier cost anything?**
This is set by the wallet provider and can vary — check your own app or the provider's official fee schedule rather than assuming it's free or assuming it costs something.

**Will a higher wallet tier increase what I can withdraw from a specific app?**
Not necessarily — the app itself may have its own separate limits on top of whatever your wallet allows. Both have to permit the amount for the transaction to go through.

**Why did my limit seem to change without me doing anything?**
Providers periodically revise their schedules of charges and limits (often quarterly) — a change on their end, not necessarily anything about your specific account.
