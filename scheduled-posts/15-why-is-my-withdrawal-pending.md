## Direct answer

A withdrawal usually shows as pending due to identity verification still processing, normal payment-processor delays, or the app's own stated processing window simply not having elapsed yet. If it exceeds that stated window with no explanation, that's when to contact support rather than keep waiting.

## The most common reasons

- **Verification isn't complete.** If a platform requires identity verification before releasing funds and yours is still pending, your withdrawal will typically hold until that clears — see our [KYC explainer](/blog/what-does-kyc-verification-mean/) for what that process generally involves.
- **Normal processing time hasn't elapsed.** Payment processors and mobile wallets have their own processing windows, which aren't instant even when everything is working correctly.
- **A payment-processor-side delay.** Occasionally a transaction is genuinely in flight on the payment rail itself, independent of anything the app or the player did.

## Pak Rummy's stated processing time

The Pak Rummy team states a typical withdrawal processing time of 5–10 minutes, with the caveat that it can take longer if identity verification is still pending. That's the figure to check your own experience against — if you're well past that window with verification already complete, that's a more specific situation than "pending is normal," and worth following up on. See our [withdrawal guide](/withdrawal/) for the current status of this figure.

## What to do if it's taking longer than expected

1. Confirm your identity verification status is actually complete, not just submitted — see [account verification](/account-verification/).
2. Compare the elapsed time against the app's stated processing window rather than your own expectation of how fast it "should" be.
3. If you're past that window with verification confirmed complete, use the app's [complaints](/complaints/) channel rather than continuing to wait indefinitely.

## FAQ

**How long is normal for a withdrawal to stay pending?**
This varies by app and payment method — check the specific processing-time figure the app itself states before assuming a problem.

**Does incomplete KYC cause a withdrawal to stay pending?**
Yes, commonly — see our [KYC explainer](/blog/what-does-kyc-verification-mean/) and [account verification](/account-verification/) page.

**When should I escalate instead of waiting?**
If a withdrawal exceeds the app's own stated processing window with no explanation and your verification is confirmed complete, that's when to use a formal complaints channel. See [complaints](/complaints/).
