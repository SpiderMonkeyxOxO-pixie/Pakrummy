## Direct answer

Real money earning games in Pakistan generally fall into three categories: skill-based card games (Rummy, Teen Patti, Poker), prediction/multiplier games (Aviator, Crash), and chance-based games (Slots). Legal status and platform trustworthiness vary and should be checked independently of game type.

## Skill-based card games

Games like Rummy, Teen Patti, and Poker combine a chance element — the cards you're dealt — with a skill element: how you play those cards. Outcomes aren't purely random; decision-making affects results over time, even though any single hand still carries uncertainty. These are the games most people mean when they refer to "card game apps" in this category.

## Prediction and multiplier games

Games like Aviator and Crash use a rising multiplier that players try to cash out before an unpredictable end point. Unlike card games, there's no decision-making skill involved beyond timing a single cash-out action — see our [Aviator](/blog/how-aviator-game-works/) and [Crash](/blog/how-crash-games-work/) explainers for how the mechanic actually works and why it can't be predicted.

## Chance-based games

Slots and similar reel-based games are purely chance-based — there's no decision that changes the odds of any individual spin. These are the most straightforwardly luck-dependent category in this space.

## What's common across all three categories

Regardless of category, every game here:

- **Involves real financial risk.** None of these categories are "free to play" in any meaningful sense once real money is staked.
- **Depends on the operator's trustworthiness, not just the game mechanic.** A fair game mechanic doesn't guarantee a trustworthy operator — those are separate questions. See our [checklist for evaluating earning apps](/blog/is-this-online-earning-app-in-pakistan-legit/).
- **Has an uncertain legal status that varies by jurisdiction and changes over time.** See our [legal status page](/legal-status/), which is explicitly not legal advice.
