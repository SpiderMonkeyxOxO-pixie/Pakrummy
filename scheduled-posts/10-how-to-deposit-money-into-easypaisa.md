## Direct answer

To deposit money into Easypaisa, open the app, select "Add Money," choose a funding source — bank transfer, debit card, or an agent cash deposit — and confirm the amount. Processing time depends on the method chosen.

## Funding sources

Easypaisa generally accepts a few ways to add money to your balance:

- **Bank transfer**, if your bank account is linked to Easypaisa.
- **Debit card**, added directly within the app.
- **Agent cash deposit**, where a physical Easypaisa agent adds funds to your account for cash.

Which of these is available to you depends on your account setup and verification level.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the Easypaisa app and select **Add Money** (or the equivalent deposit option).
2. Choose your funding source — bank transfer, card, or agent.
3. Enter the amount you want to deposit.
4. Confirm the transaction, including any PIN or OTP step Easypaisa requires.
5. Wait for confirmation — bank transfers and card deposits are often near-instant, while agent deposits depend on the agent processing it on their end.

## Minimums, limits, and fees

Minimum deposit amounts, maximum limits, and any fees are set by Easypaisa and vary by account tier and funding method — not by any specific app you might later use that balance with. Check the figures shown directly in your own Easypaisa app rather than relying on a number you've seen elsewhere, since these can change.

## If your deposit doesn't reflect right away

Don't assume a deposit has failed just because it hasn't appeared instantly — processing time varies by method, and a short delay is often normal rather than a sign something went wrong.
