## Direct answer

Easypaisa withdrawal charges vary by transaction type, destination, and the receiving app's own fee policy — there is no single flat rate. Always confirm the exact charge shown on your own withdrawal screen before confirming.

## Why there's no single answer

Easypaisa processes withdrawals to a range of destinations — bank accounts, other wallets, cash-out through an agent — and each path can carry a different fee structure, sometimes tiered by the amount being withdrawn. On top of that, the app you're withdrawing *from* can apply its own separate processing fee before the money ever reaches Easypaisa. Any flat number you see quoted online, including on this page if we ever publish one, should be treated as a starting point, not a guarantee — the number that actually matters is the one shown on your own screen at the moment of withdrawal.

## What determines the charge

- **Transaction amount.** Many wallets apply tiered rates, where the percentage or flat fee changes at certain amount thresholds.
- **Destination.** Withdrawing to a bank account, another wallet, or cash via an agent can each carry different charges.
- **The app's own fee policy.** Separate from Easypaisa's own charges, the app facilitating the withdrawal may apply its own fee on top.

## What to check before you withdraw

1. Look at the exact fee shown on the confirmation screen before you approve the transaction — not a figure you remember from last time, since rates can change.
2. If the amount that arrives is lower than expected, check whether the gap matches a disclosed fee or looks unexplained — the two call for different next steps.
3. Keep your transaction ID and timestamp until the withdrawal is confirmed and reflects in your account.
