## Direct answer

KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is the identity-check process gaming apps use to confirm who holds an account, typically requiring a government-issued ID, before releasing withdrawals. It exists to reduce fraud and protect both the player and the platform.

## Why gaming apps require KYC at all

Real-money platforms sit between a player's identity and their money, which makes account takeover and fraud a real risk on both sides — for the platform, and for the player if someone else gains access to their account. KYC exists to tie an account to a verified real person before significant money moves out, which reduces fraud risk and is standard practice across real-money platforms generally, not something specific to one app.

## What documents are typically requested

Requirements vary by platform, but commonly include:

- A government-issued ID — in Pakistan, typically a CNIC.
- Sometimes a selfie or a photo holding the ID, to confirm the document matches the account holder.
- Occasionally, proof of the payment method being used (like a screenshot confirming account ownership).

Exactly which of these apply to any specific app isn't something we're asserting here beyond what we've separately confirmed — see our [account verification page](/account-verification/) for Pak Rummy's specific, confirmed process.

## When KYC typically applies: before play, or only before withdrawal?

This varies by platform. Some apps require verification before you can even register or deposit; others let you play first and only require it when you attempt your first withdrawal. Neither approach is universal, so check the specific app's own flow rather than assuming.

## What happens if you skip it

If an app requires KYC before withdrawal and you haven't completed it, your withdrawal will typically be held or rejected until verification is finished — this is a common, often-misunderstood cause of a withdrawal appearing "stuck," separate from any technical issue or fee question.

## FAQ

**Why do gaming apps require KYC at all?**
To confirm the account holder's identity before releasing funds, reducing fraud and account-takeover risk for both the player and the platform.

**What documents are typically requested?**
A government-issued ID (such as a CNIC in Pakistan) and sometimes a selfie or proof-of-ownership step — exact requirements vary by app.

**Does KYC have to happen before I can play, or only before I withdraw?**
This varies by app; many allow play before verification but require it before the first withdrawal.
