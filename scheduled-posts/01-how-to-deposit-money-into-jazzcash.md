## Direct answer

To deposit money into JazzCash, open the app, select "Add Money" (or "Load Wallet"), choose a funding source — bank transfer, debit/credit card, or an agent cash deposit — and confirm the amount. Processing time depends on the method chosen.

## Funding sources

JazzCash generally accepts a few ways to add money to your wallet balance:

- **Bank transfer**, if your bank account is linked to JazzCash through its "My JazzCash" or bank-linking flow.
- **Debit or credit card**, added directly within the app.
- **Agent cash deposit**, where a physical JazzCash agent (a "Mobilink Microfinance Bank" retail point) adds funds to your account for cash.

Which of these is available to you depends on your account setup and verification level.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the JazzCash app and select **Add Money** or **Load Wallet**.
2. Choose your funding source — bank transfer, card, or agent.
3. Enter the amount you want to deposit.
4. Confirm the transaction, including any PIN or OTP step JazzCash requires.
5. Wait for confirmation — bank transfers and card deposits are often near-instant, while agent deposits depend on the agent processing it on their end.

## Minimums, limits, and fees

Minimum deposit amounts, maximum limits, and any fees are set by JazzCash and vary by account tier and funding method — not by any specific app you might later use that balance with. Check the figures shown directly in your own JazzCash app, or JazzCash's own published schedule of charges, rather than relying on a number you've seen elsewhere, since these can change.

## If your deposit doesn't reflect right away

Don't assume a deposit has failed just because it hasn't appeared instantly — processing time varies by method, and a short delay is often normal rather than a sign something went wrong. See our guide on [delayed mobile wallet deposits](/guides/mobile-wallet-deposit-delay/) before concluding a transaction failed.
