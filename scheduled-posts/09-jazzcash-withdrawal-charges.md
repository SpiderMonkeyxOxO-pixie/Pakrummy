## Direct answer

JazzCash withdrawal charges depend on the transaction amount, destination, and the receiving app's fee policy, and are not a single fixed rate. Confirm the exact charge on your withdrawal screen, and note that identity verification status can delay a withdrawal independently of any fee.

## Why there's no single answer

Like most mobile wallets, JazzCash's withdrawal fees can vary by how much you're withdrawing, where it's going (a bank account, another wallet, a cash-out agent), and whether your account is a basic mobile account or one linked to a CNIC with fuller verification. The app initiating the withdrawal can also apply its own separate charge. The only number worth trusting is the one shown to you at the moment of the transaction.

## What determines the charge

- **Amount tier.** Many wallets apply different rates above or below certain thresholds.
- **Destination.** Bank transfer, inter-wallet transfer, and agent cash-out can each carry different fees.
- **Account type.** A basic mobile wallet and a fully CNIC-verified JazzCash account can have different transaction limits and fee treatment.

## A JazzCash-specific thing worth knowing

JazzCash accounts have tiers tied to how much identity information you've provided — a basic account typically has lower transaction limits than a fully verified one. If a withdrawal is capped or behaving unexpectedly, it's worth checking your account tier before assuming it's purely a fee issue. See our [account verification page](/account-verification/) for what identity verification generally involves.

## What to check before you withdraw

1. Confirm the exact fee on the transaction screen before approving it.
2. If a withdrawal is delayed or capped, check whether your account's verification tier is the actual cause before assuming a fee problem.
3. Keep your transaction ID and timestamp until the withdrawal is confirmed.
