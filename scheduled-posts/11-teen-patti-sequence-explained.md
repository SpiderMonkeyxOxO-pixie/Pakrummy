## Direct answer

Teen Patti hand sequence, highest to lowest: Trail, Pure Sequence, Sequence, Color, Pair, and High Card. Ties within the same hand type are broken by comparing card rank, highest first.

## The full ranking, with examples

| Rank | Hand | Example | Why it ranks here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Trail** (three of a kind) | 9♠ 9♥ 9♦ | The rarest possible hand — three cards of identical rank |
| 2 | **Pure Sequence** | 5♣ 6♣ 7♣ | Three consecutive cards, all the same suit |
| 3 | **Sequence** | 5♣ 6♥ 7♦ | Three consecutive cards, mixed suits |
| 4 | **Color** | 2♠ 7♠ J♠ | Three cards of the same suit, not consecutive |
| 5 | **Pair** | 10♦ 10♣ 4♥ | Two cards of matching rank, plus one unrelated card |
| 6 | **High Card** | K♠ 8♥ 3♦ | None of the above — compare the highest card |

## How ties are broken

**Within a Trail:** the higher rank wins — three Kings beats three Nines. Aces rank highest here (A-A-A is the best possible Trail).

**Within a Pure Sequence or Sequence:** compare the highest card in each sequence. A-2-3 is the lowest possible sequence (Ace ranks low here), while Q-K-A ranks highest.

**Within a Color:** compare the highest card first, then the next-highest, and so on if still tied.

**Within a Pair:** the higher pair wins first; if pairs match, the unrelated third card (the "kicker") decides it.

**Within High Card:** compare the highest card, then the next, continuing down until the tie breaks.

## Does suit matter?

Suit only matters for determining whether a hand qualifies as a Pure Sequence or a Color in the first place — it does not break ties between two hands of the same rank once the hand type is already decided. A Pure Sequence in spades and a Pure Sequence in hearts of the same card values are equally ranked; the comparison moves to card rank, not suit.

## FAQ

**Does suit matter when comparing two sequences?**
No — a sequence beats a color regardless of suit; only the hand type ranking matters, then card rank as a tiebreaker.

**Is A-2-3 a valid sequence?**
Yes. Ace can rank low at the bottom of a sequence (A-2-3), but ranks highest in a Trail (A-A-A).

**What breaks a tie between two Trails?**
Compare the rank of the three matching cards — the higher rank wins (for example, 9-9-9 beats 7-7-7).
